# Music licenses

Soccer x Scripture uses **licensed** library music (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or similar). Do not rip Premier League intros, FIFA OSTs, or unlicensed tracks.

## What to store here

- PDF or screenshot **receipts** for each track used in a published Reel
- Optional: a one-line note `YYYY-MM-DD | plan_id | track title | library`

**Do not commit audio files** (`.mp3` / `.wav` stay gitignored under `assets/music/`).

## Workflow

1. Open the edit recipe — use `music.suggested_search` and `bpm_target`
2. Pick an instrumental: stadium / trap-adjacent / cinematic drums, ~120–140 BPM
3. Download for social use under your subscription terms
4. Drop the receipt into this folder before posting
5. Place the track in Resolve on the music lane; keep the **outro stinger** consistent
